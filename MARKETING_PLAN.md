# AppLens Marketing Plan

## Product Overview

AppLens is an SDK that enables React Native/Expo developers to add AI-powered app review functionality to their mobile apps. The AI navigates through apps, captures screenshots, and humans review and mark issues — creating a powerful QA and feedback loop directly within the app.

## 2. Key Value Propositions

- **AI-Navigated Exploration** — The AI autonomously walks through your app, discovering screens and flows you might not have tested, surfacing issues users actually encounter.

- **Visual Bug Reports** — Instead of text-only feedback, get screenshots + step-by-step reproduction paths. Developers love this; no more guessing what "the blue button" refers to.

- **Seamless React Native / Expo Integration** — Drop-in SDK with just a few lines of code. Full TypeScript support, works with Expo (managed or bare).

- **Human-in-the-Loop Review** — AI does the heavy lifting; your QA team reviews and validates. Combines automation with human judgment for accurate prioritization.

- **Prioritized Issue Tracking** — Issues are scored and categorized automatically, so your team focuses on what matters most instead of triaging noise.

## 4. Developer Community Channels to Target

### Primary Channels

| Channel | Why Target | How to Engage |

| **React Native Discord** (RN Discord, Expo Discord) | Direct access to core RN/Expo dev audience | Share use cases, sponsor #showcase or #help channels |

| **DEV.to** | Dev blogging community, great for SEO | Publish tutorials: "How to add in-app QA to your Expo app in 5 minutes" |
| **Hashnode** | Similar to DEV.to, tech-focused | Guest posts, AppLens blog |

### Secondary Channels

- **Stack Overflow** — Answer questions with AppLens solutions, build SEO presence
- **LinkedIn** — Target mobile app development agencies and QA managers
- **Product Hunt** — Launch day visibility for developer tools

- Show, don't tell: share demo videos and code snippets
